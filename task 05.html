LGU IEEE STUDENT BRANCH

The IEEE Student Branch at Lahore Garrison University (LGU) is a dynamic platform promoting technical innovation and professional growth. It connects students with a global engineering network through workshops, seminars, and international competitions. The branch empowers members by building essential leadership, project management, and cutting-edge technical skills.
